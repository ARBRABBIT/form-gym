The user-provided orange scale photo is the subject/material reference only: retain its square softly rounded body, brushed chrome perimeter, subtly diamond-textured rubber standing pad, and inset mechanical dial at the upper center. Adapt the rubber to pale pistachio/lime green (#dcf2ac), with warm ivory and restrained charcoal details to match an existing white/soft-gray/lime app. Front-facing orthographic top-down view, no diagonal rotation or perspective skew; realistic beveled depth, substantial chrome rim, beautiful soft studio lighting from upper left and soft short contact shadow. Center the entire scale on a square transparent canvas with about 8% clear margin all around. Crucial functional compositing constraint: the dial is a clean BLANK ivory rectangular recessed window at the upper center, approximately from x=24% to 76% and y=15% to 39% of the image canvas, with a thin chrome beveled surround. Absolutely NO numbers, NO needle, NO tick marks, NO words and NO logos anywhere: a real interactive dial will be drawn in code inside that recess. Keep the rubber body texture subtle and realistic, not cartoon/plastic toy. Isolated object on genuinely transparent background, no colored background or floor plane. Crisp product quality.
