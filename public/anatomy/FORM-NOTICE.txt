Form workout muscle viewer

Named, authored muscle-part meshes grouped by anatomical name. No position masks or projected region cuts. Muscle and tendon materials remain separate.
Muscles and supporting bones, cartilage, teeth and eye geometry selected. Nonselectable supporting structures combined; mapped muscle parts remain separate and named. Materials replaced, world scale centered to 2 units, geometry simplified with bounded error and locked borders, and Meshopt compressed. Inner-ear, kidney and other organ systems are excluded.

Primary muscles are green; assisting muscles are lighter green. Planned focus is previewed before any sets are recorded; recorded sets determine the map after training begins. Highlights describe exercise associations, not measured muscle activation or recovery.

Rebuild: BLENDER_BIN=/path/to/blender npm run prepare:anatomy
Blender is only needed to rebuild the model, not to run or deploy the app.

Athletic shape adaptation: A smooth, symmetrical whole-body deformation broadens the shoulder girdle and adds chest, upper-arm, thigh and calf volume. A relaxed A-pose opens the arms by 25 degrees and the legs by 10 degrees from the source stance. All tissues share the same deformation so muscle attachments and authored boundaries remain together. Head and foot shapes are preserved. This is a stylized fitness visualization, not a clinical anatomy reference.
